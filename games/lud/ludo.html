How to play
ObjectiveBring all four of your tokens from home to the center before everyone else.
Starting tokensYour four tokens begin inside your colored home area.
DiceOn your turn, roll once and move one highlighted legal token by that exact number.
MovementMove around the outside path, then into your own colored home lane.
Bringing tokens outRoll a 6 to move a token from home onto its matching entry square.
Safe squaresTokens on marked safe squares cannot be captured.
CapturingLand on an opponent on a non-safe square to send that token back home.
Home laneOnly your own color can enter your lane toward the center.
FinishingYou need the exact roll to move a token into the center.
WinningThe first player to finish all four tokens wins.
Special six ruleRolling a 6 gives you another turn after that move.
Three sixes ruleThree 6s in a row end that player’s turn.
Current mode rulesClassic uses standard Ludo. Chaos adds snakes, ladders, and a bonus coin tile. Power adds special tiles. Battle emphasizes captures and blocks. Arena combines advanced events.
