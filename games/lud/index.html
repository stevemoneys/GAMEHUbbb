How To Play

Roll a 6 to bring a token from home to entry.

Move clockwise by the exact dice value.

Capture opponents on non-safe squares.

Reach center with all 4 tokens to win.

Classic Notes

Three 6s in a row loses the turn.

Exact movement is needed in the home lane.
