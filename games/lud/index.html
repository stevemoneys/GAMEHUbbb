Quick Guide

Learn the board, make every roll count, and bring all four tokens home.

Objective

Move all four of your tokens from home to the center before the other players.

Starting the game

Players take turns in order. The active player rolls the dice.

Rolling the dice

Roll once, then choose one highlighted legal token to move.

Leave home

Roll a 6 to bring a token from its home area onto its matching entry square.

Move a token

Move clockwise by the exact number rolled. A token cannot make an illegal move.

Capture opponents

Land on an opponent on a non-safe square to send that token back home.

Safe squares

Tokens on safe squares cannot be captured by another player.

Home lane

After circling the board, enter only your own color lane toward the center.

Reach the center

You need the exact roll to finish a token at the center.

Winning

The first player to finish all four tokens wins the match.

11. Three sixes

Three consecutive rolls of 6 end that player’s turn.

12. Mode rules
