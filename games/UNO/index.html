UNO Master Guide

How To Play

1. Goal Of UNO

Your objective is to be the first player to get rid of all cards in your hand. You do this by matching the top discard card by color or value, while using action cards to control turns and pressure opponents.

2. Match Setup

Every player starts with seven cards. One card is placed face up as the first discard card. The remaining cards form the draw pile in the center.

3. Core Turn Rules

On your turn, play one valid card that matches either the discard color or value. If you have no valid move, draw from the draw pile. Turn order normally moves to the next player unless altered by action cards.

4. Action Cards

  • Skip: Next player loses their turn.
  • Reverse: Turn direction changes. In 1v1 flow, Reverse behaves like an extra skip effect.
  • Draw Two (+2): Next player draws two and loses turn unless stack rules continue it.
  • Wild: You choose the next active color.
  • Wild Draw Four (+4): You choose color and the next player draws four unless stacking continues.

5. UNO Call Timing

When you drop to one card, press UNO before your turn flow advances. Calling it on time protects your play and keeps your final-card push safe.

6. Game Modes In This App

  • Tournament: Classic four-player table with its own progression road.
  • Quick Play: Fast 1v1 match against one AI rival.
  • 2V2: You and an AI teammate versus two AI opponents. Your side wins if either teammate finishes first.

7. Winning And Progression

A round ends when one side empties its hand. Winning advances level progression for the active mode and unlocks harder tables over time.

8. Practical Strategy Tips

  • Keep at least one flexible color option whenever possible.
  • Use action cards to protect yourself when an opponent is low on cards.
  • Delay wild cards if you still have natural matches available.
  • Watch card counts and save disruption cards for final turns.
  • In 2V2, prioritize team survival over individual hand speed.
