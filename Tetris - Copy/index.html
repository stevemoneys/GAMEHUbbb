How To Play

Clear lines, build combos, and use power-ups at the right moment.

Goal

Fill each row with blocks to clear it. In stage modes, clear the target lines to win.

Mobile Touch Controls

  • Tap left side: move left.
  • Tap right side: move right.
  • Tap center: rotate piece.
  • Swipe down: drop faster.
  • Swipe up: hold piece.

Quick Tips

  • Keep the center low and avoid tall stacks.
  • Use hold to save an I piece for tight gaps.
  • Use power-ups when a line clear is guaranteed.
